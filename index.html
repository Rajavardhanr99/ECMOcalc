Method

Flow (L/min) = Weight × Rate ÷ 1000
  • Weight = the dosing weight chosen in the patient bar (kg); rate in mL/kg/min
  • In obesity many centres index to ideal or adjusted body weight — flow is driven by cardiac output and VO₂, not fat mass.
BSA = 0.007184 × H^0.725 × W^0.425 Predicted CO = BSA × CI Target flow = Predicted CO × support % Support % = Current flow ÷ Predicted CO
  • Default 3.0 L/min/m² × 80 % ≈ 2.4 L/min/m² — the usual initial VA target.
  • “Support %” is relative to predicted resting output; actual native output is unknown and varies.

Blood is not water

Manufacturers test with water. In whole blood (Hct ≈ 27 %, 37 °C) Broman et al. found:

  • Return cannulae: flows usually 3–21 % below the charts; 20–90 % more pressure gradient for the same flow, worst in 15–17 Fr.
  • Drainage cannulae: drainage pressure 10–350 % higher than the charts at a given flow.
  • Dual-lumen: drainage needed 10–80 % more pressure drop; return similar to the charts.

Treat these values as a best case. Length, side-hole position, haematocrit, vessel size and tip position all change real-world performance.

Targets in common use

  • Anti-Xa 0.3–0.7 IU/mL (ELSO); ISTH suggests 0.3–0.5

Recirculation

VV ECMO. Uses pre/post saturations and SvO₂ from the other cards.

Recirculation fraction—
Effective flow—
R = (SpreO₂ − SvO₂) ÷ (SpostO₂ − SvO₂) Effective flow = ECMO flow × (1 − R)

SvO₂ should reflect true venous return uncontaminated by ECMO return blood — on VV ECMO this is hard to obtain, so treat the result as an estimate and trend it.

Sweep for target PaCO₂

CO₂ removal ≈ proportional to sweep.

Estimated sweep—
New sweep ≈ Sweep × PaCO₂ ÷ Target

Less accurate at high sweep or low blood flow, and ignores native lung CO₂ clearance. Avoid rapid PaCO₂ falls, especially at ECMO initiation.

Predicted SaO₂ on VV ECMO

Steady-state mixing model. ECMO-oxygenated blood mixes with venous return, passes the native lung (shunt), and reaches the arteries; oxygen consumption then sets venous content, which feeds back to the circuit.

Model

Qeff = QECMO × (1 − R), capped at CO CpaO₂ = [Qeff·Cpost + (CO − Qeff)·CvO₂] ÷ CO CaO₂ = Qs/Qt·CpaO₂ + (1 − Qs/Qt)·Cc′O₂ CvO₂ = CaO₂ − VO₂ ÷ (10·CO) (solved iteratively) PAO₂ = FiO₂ × (760 − 47) − 40 ÷ 0.8 S = 1 ÷ [23 400 ÷ (P³ + 150P) + 1] (Severinghaus)
  • Schmidt et al.: when ECMO flow ÷ cardiac output exceeded 60 %, SaO₂ was always above 90 %.
  • Assumes sea level, PaCO₂ 40 mmHg, full end-capillary equilibration in ventilated alveoli and no right-to-left cardiac shunt.
